## Notas para la revisión

The app works fully without an account. Signing in is optional and only syncs favorites, My bar, the shopping list and settings between devices. To try it, go to the Account tab, enter any email address and type the 6-digit code that arrives by email. Account deletion is in the Account tab ("Delete my account").

The app shows alcoholic drink recipes. It opens with an age check (21 or older) and responsible drinking messages. There are no sales of alcohol.
